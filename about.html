About NorCalHunt

We're hunters first.

We're just two local boys who grew up chasing game across Northern California. From blacktail deer in the timber to waterfowl in the marsh, hunting has been a way of life for us for more than 50 years. Along the way, we've built lasting friendships, trained great hunting dogs, and collected stories that can only be earned through time spent outdoors.

Like many hunters, we grew tired of bouncing between websites to find the information we needed before every trip. Weather was on one site, regulations on another, sunrise and sunset times somewhere else, and gear recommendations scattered all over the internet.

We believed there had to be a better way.

So we built NorCalHunt — a place where hunters can find the tools and information they rely on before every hunt. Whether you're checking the weather, reviewing California hunting regulations, looking up shooting and solunar times, researching products, or planning your next deer, waterfowl, upland bird, or predator hunt, our goal is to put everything at your fingertips.
